Community Policing Practice Assessment

Stratford Police Department · Prepared by CARE

CARE is looking to understand what current practices are or are not in place. We researched community policing practices from across the country and gathered this list. It is our understanding that many of these practices are already used by the Stratford Police Department, so this is simply an effort to understand what is currently in place.
Definition

Community policing is a department-wide way of working in which officers use long-term partnerships with residents, businesses, and local organizations, plus structured problem-solving, to proactively reduce specific crime, disorder, and fear conditions in identified places. These practices are managed and resourced by the department and all officers are evaluated on that work.
